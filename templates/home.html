How it works

Upload / enter

Drop a lab report or type values. Missing values are allowed.

Verify

Extracted values are shown in the form so you can correct them.

Predict & explain

Calibrated model → probability, risk, eGFR stage and top factors.

Track & report

Saved to history with trend charts and a PDF report.

⚕️ CKDPredict is a screening / decision-support aid. It assists in interpreting multiple clinical parameters together and requires professional medical confirmation. It does not replace a doctor or laboratory diagnosis.
